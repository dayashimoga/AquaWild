## 2. Subphase 1.1.1 — Comprehensive Discovery & Gap Analysis Report (Completed)

### 2.1 Visual & Functional Flaws Identified from Screenshots & Code Audit

1. **[CRITICAL-01] Launch Screen Unrealistic Balloon Floating & Soap-Bubble Clutter (`Screenshot 1: #/launch`)**
   - **Evidence**:
     - `lib/src/presentation/widgets/pool/pool_water_simulation_painter.dart` (lines 101–138): `_drawCaustics()` renders a uniform $36\text{px}$ grid of `addOval(Rect.fromCircle(...))` across the entire viewport, producing hundreds of repetitive white circle outlines that look like floating soap bubbles/balloons rather than organic water refraction caustics.
     - `lib/src/presentation/widgets/pool/floating_red_panda_painter.dart` (lines 110–127, 160–212): Renders the Red Panda inside an artificial coral/teal inflatable ring tube (`_drawSwimTubeBack`, `_drawSwimTubeFront`) bobbing vertically in place.
     - `lib/src/presentation/widgets/pool/interactive_pool_view.dart` (lines 92, 128–134, 164–182): Red Panda is hardcoded at `Offset(size.width / 2, size.height * 0.42)`. Tapping the canvas does not move the Red Panda; there is no tap/click-to-move pathfinding, no anticipation crouch, no momentum/easing, no landing animation, and no natural idle breathing, blinking, tail wagging, or eye tracking.

2. **[CRITICAL-02] Gaps Between Stratified Fluid Layers (`Screenshot 2: #/home`)**
   - **Evidence**:
     - `lib/src/presentation/widgets/animal_fluid/animal_fluid_vessel_painter.dart` (lines 158–215): In `_drawStratifiedFluid()`, each layer `l` draws its bottom boundary as a flat horizontal line (`layerPath.moveTo(0, currentBaseY); ... layerPath.lineTo(size.width, currentBaseY);`), while layer `l - 1` directly below it draws its top boundary with a sinusoidal wave (`layerTopY + sway` where `sway = math.sin(...) * 2.5`). Whenever `sway > 0`, the lower layer dips below `currentBaseY`, leaving dark crescent background gaps between adjacent beverage layers!

3. **[CRITICAL-03] Anatomically Inaccurate Red Panda Home Vessel Silhouette & Missing Facial Features (`Screenshot 2: #/home`)**
   - **Evidence**:
     - `lib/src/presentation/widgets/animal_fluid/animal_silhouette_paths.dart` (lines 38–61): `_buildRedPandaPath()` uses sharp polygonal `lineTo` vertices (`lineTo(w * 0.28, h * 0.28)`, `lineTo(w * 0.22, h * 0.12)`), looking like a rigid geometric cat cutout.
     - `lib/src/presentation/widgets/animal_fluid/animal_fluid_vessel_painter.dart` (lines 528–556): `_drawRedPandaExteriorDetails()` draws two solid white circles on the ears (`drawCircle(Offset(w * 0.29, h * 0.17), w * 0.045)`), **zero eyes** (only two horizontal whisker lines that look like closed slits), and a dark `#212121` nose dot that disappears against the dark empty vessel background.

4. **[HIGH-04] Exotic Animal Ecosystem Consistency, Alpha Layering & ID Mismatch (`Screenshot 3: Companions Modal`)**
   - **Evidence**:
     - `lib/src/domain/models/exotic_animal.dart` (lines 48–51 vs 149–161): `avatarEmoji` checks `case 'pink_river_dolphin':` and `case 'bioluminescent_octopus':`, whereas the actual catalog IDs are `'bottlenose_dolphin'` and `'blue_ringed_octopus'`, causing both species to fall back to `'🐾'`.
     - All 10 exotic animals in `AnimalFluidVesselPainter` lack shared anatomical alpha-underlay shading, blinking/breathing idle life-cycles, eye tracking, and custom vector avatars in `BiomeCompanionSelectorSheet`.

5. **[CRITICAL-05] Missing Premium Monetization, Subscriptions, IAPs & Expansion Framework**
   - **Evidence**:
     - `lib/src/presentation/widgets/biomes/biome_companion_selector_sheet.dart` (lines 224–227): Locked companions display only static text `'🔒 ${animal.unlockXp} XP'` with no tap action, no subscription tier (`AquaWild Pro`), no IAP unlock packs, no premium themes/effects/animation packs, and no storefront or entitlement management system.

6. **[CRITICAL-06] Missing Out-of-App Notifications, Home/Lock Screen Widgets & Budget Left Telemetry**
   - **Evidence**:
     - `lib/src/core/services/smart_reminder_service.dart` only computes static circadian time slots. There is no rich notification payload or home-screen/lock-screen widget engine exposing water consumed, remaining goal, daily budget left (hydration/caffeine/electrolyte budget), streaks, achievements, unlock progress, and quick-log actions without opening the app.

7. **[CRITICAL-07] Missing Admin App / Command Portal (`/admin`)**
   - **Evidence**:
     - `lib/src/core/router/app_router.dart` only defines `/launch` and `/home`. There is no `/admin` portal with all features unlocked (God Mode) nor management modules for users, content catalog, analytics, monetization SKUs, notifications, remote configuration, feature flags, environment switching, or immutable audit logs.

8. **[HIGH-08] Stale AI Hydration Coach Telemetry & Desktop Layout Clipping (`Screenshot 2: #/home`)**
   - **Evidence**:
     - `lib/src/presentation/controllers/ai_coach_controller.dart` (lines 50–55): `AiCoachNotifier.build()` uses `ref.read(hydrationProvider)` once on startup and does not listen to `hydrationProvider` mutations, causing the right-hand AI Coach card to display `"You are currently at 0% of your target with 2500ml remaining"` even when the user is at `1639 / 2500 ml (66%)`.
     - `lib/src/presentation/screens/home/responsive_home_shell.dart`: In the desktop center pane, the top of `_buildNetHydrationCard` (`1639 / 2500 ml` and the `66%` circular progress ring) can clip at the top viewport edge, and the horizontal `Quick Log Beverage` strip cuts off the rightmost cards without scroll arrows or a visible desktop scrollbar.
