# Architecture Decision Records (ADRs 006–009)

## ADR-006: Kinematic Red Panda Rig with Tap/Click-to-Move Pathfinding, Natural Idles & 60+ FPS Physics
- **Status**: Accepted
- **Date**: 2026-11-22
- **Owners**: `animation-agent`, `ux-ui-agent`, `solution-architect`
- **Context**: 
  - The initial launch screen (`#/launch`) placed Ruby the Red Panda inside an artificial coral/teal inflatable swim ring ("balloon floating") on a static center coordinate (`size.width / 2, size.height * 0.42`), surrounded by hundreds of repetitive $36\text{px}$ white circle outlines that resemble soap bubbles rather than organic water refraction caustics.
  - Tapping or clicking the canvas did not move the mascot; there was no tap/click-to-move locomotion, no pathfinding, no momentum/easing, and no landing animation.
  - Furthermore, the Red Panda lacked natural idle life-cycles (breathing chest expansion, blinking eyelids, multi-joint ringed tail wagging, ear twitches, eye tracking, and expressive touch reactions).
- **Decision**:
  1. **Anatomical Vector Redesign**: Replace the cartoonish swim-tube and sunglasses with an anatomically accurate, multi-layered vector rig of *Ailurus fulgens* lounging naturally over crystalline highland waters with realistic fur shading, tear-track facial markings, tufted ears, and a multi-segmented ringed tail.
  2. **Organic Water Caustics**: Replace the repetitive $36\text{px}$ circular bubble grid with continuous procedural Simplex/sinusoidal caustic ribbon networks that dynamically distort with water depth and surface wave velocity.
  3. **Kinematic Locomotion Controller**:
     - Implement a 60/120 FPS spring-damper kinematic controller (`KinematicPandaController`) supporting tap/click-to-move navigation.
     - Sequence motion into 4 discrete physical phases:
       * **Anticipation**: Brief 120ms crouching compression toward the jump vector.
       * **Pathfinding & Propulsion**: Smooth cubic Hermite spline interpolation toward the destination with momentum tilt ($\theta = \text{clamp}(v_x \cdot 0.08, -0.25, 0.25)$ rad) and banking.
       * **Trailing Wake Ripples**: Emitter drops dynamic trailing water ripples and wake foam along the travel vector.
       * **Landing & Settling**: Decelerating bounce with harmonic damping ($k=0.08, d=0.12$) and splash wave excitation upon arrival.
  4. **Multi-Track Idle Life-Cycle Engine**:
     - **Breathing**: Sinusoidal torso/chest volume expansion ($T = 3.2$s, $\Delta = 4\%$).
     - **Blinking**: Poisson-distributed involuntary blinking eyelids every 3.5–6.0 seconds with smooth 150ms blink ease.
     - **Ringed Tail Wagging**: Dual-joint pendulum oscillation with fluid drag inertia.
     - **Eye & Head Tracking**: Pupils and head orient smoothly toward pointer hover coordinates (desktop/web) or recent tap locations (mobile/tablet).
     - **Expressive Touch Reactions**: Decaying physical impulse triggers playful ear twitches and joyful squint reactions.
  5. **Accessibility**: When `MediaQuery.disableAnimationsOf(context)` is true, locomotion jumps instantly without motion sickness, and continuous idles settle into a gentle static pose.
- **Consequences**: Eliminates artificial balloon floating, delivers AAA-grade mascot vitality at 60/120 FPS with zero garbage collection allocations, and provides playful interactive locomotion.

## ADR-007: Zero-Gap Stratified Fluid Wave Mesh Architecture with Specular Interfacial Meniscus
- **Status**: Accepted
- **Date**: 2026-11-22
- **Owners**: `animation-agent`, `solution-architect`, `qa-agent`
- **Context**:
  - In `AnimalFluidVesselPainter`, each beverage stratum $l$ rendered its bottom boundary with a flat horizontal line at `currentBaseY`, while layer $l-1$ directly below it rendered its top surface with a sinusoidal wave (`layerTopY + sway`).
  - Whenever the lower wave's trough dipped below `currentBaseY`, a dark background gap appeared between adjacent beverage layers, breaking the visual illusion of a continuous column of liquid.
- **Decision**:
  1. **Coupled Boundary Mesh Arrays**:
     - Pre-compute $K+1$ discrete 64-column wave elevation arrays $W_0, W_1, \dots, W_K$, where $W_0$ is the vessel floor ($y = \text{height}$) and $W_K$ is the top liquid surface driven by `WaveColumnSolver`.
     - Subsurface interfaces ($W_1$ to $W_{K-1}$) derive from physical density differentials and wave energy attenuation:
       $$W_l(x, t) = \text{baseY}_l + \text{solverWave}(x) \cdot \left(\frac{l}{K}\right)^{1.4} + \sin\left(\frac{x}{28} + \omega t + l\right) \cdot A_l$$
  2. **Mathematically Locked Layer Polygons**:
     - Construct the `Path` for stratum $l$ by:
       1. Traversing bottom interface $W_{l-1}$ in reverse from $x=\text{width} \to 0$.
       2. Traversing top interface $W_l$ forward from $x=0 \to \text{width}$.
       3. Closing the path back to the start.
     - This guarantees that layer $l$'s bottom edge and layer $l-1$'s top edge share **identical float vertex coordinates**, mathematically eliminating sub-pixel and multi-pixel gaps.
  3. **Specular Interfacial Meniscus Highlights**:
     - Along each shared interface $W_l$, render a thin ($1.2\text{px}$), translucent specular meniscus line with refractive alpha-blending (`Color.alphaBlend(stratumColor, Colors.white)`), preserving crisp, realistic separation between different liquids (e.g. coffee settling beneath water).
- **Consequences**: Completely eliminates visual gaps between beverage layers while maintaining fluid density stratification, real-time wave sloshing, and high-DPI visual clarity.

## ADR-008: Universal Monetization Entitlement Engine, Dual-Path Unlocks & Storefront Architecture
- **Status**: Accepted
- **Date**: 2026-11-22
- **Owners**: `product-agent`, `solution-architect`, `security-agent`
- **Context**:
  - Existing animal companions only support XP unlocks via drinking water (`🔒 1300 XP`), with no monetization, subscriptions, IAPs, or premium expansion features.
  - The app requires a production-ready monetization engine supporting subscriptions (AquaWild Pro), IAPs, unlock packs, premium themes, particle effects, and animation packs with seamless cross-platform execution (including web and desktop sandboxes).
- **Decision**:
  1. **Monetization Domain & Entitlements Hierarchy**:
     - Build `MonetizationController` and `MonetizationState` managing active subscriptions, purchased one-time IAP products, and dynamic entitlements.
     - Establish a strict deterministic unlock evaluation priority:
       $$\text{IsUnlocked}(animal) = \text{AdminGodMode} \lor \text{AquaWildProActive} \lor \text{PackPurchased}(animal) \lor (\text{UserXP} \ge \text{unlockXp})$$
  2. **SKU Catalog & Expansion Framework**:
     - **Subscriptions**: `aquawild_pro_monthly` (\$3.99/mo), `aquawild_pro_annual` (\$29.99/yr with 7-day trial), `aquawild_pro_lifetime` (\$69.99).
     - **Animal Unlock Packs**: *Deep Sea Pack* (Otter, Dolphin, Octopus), *Alpine Legends Pack* (Llama, Fox, Penguin, Snow Leopard).
     - **Cosmetic & Animation Packs**: *Neon Bioluminescence Shader Pack*, *Thermal Onsen Steam & Sakura FX*, *Zen Flute Soundscape Pack*.
     - **Seasonal Hydration Pass**: Summer Hydration Splash Pass & Winter Frost Expedition.
  3. **Storefront UX & Dual-Path Unlocks**:
     - Design glassmorphic `PremiumStorefrontModal` and contextual unlock sheets.
     - In `BiomeCompanionSelectorSheet`, locked animals offer dual paths: earn via daily hydration XP or unlock instantly via AquaWild Pro / Pack IAP.
  4. **Offline Resilience & Purchase Verification**:
     - Store entitlements locally in encrypted persistent state with cryptographic signature verification.
     - Provide a 14-day offline grace period for active subscriptions before requiring cloud re-validation.
     - Include full sandbox/simulation mode for Web and Desktop platforms where native App Store / Google Play billing APIs are unavailable.
- **Consequences**: Creates a recurring revenue engine, rewards free users through organic XP progression, and provides an extensible foundation for future content releases.

## ADR-009: Out-of-App Hydration/Budget Telemetry, Rich Notifications, Home/Lock-Screen Widgets & Unified Admin Command Portal (`/admin`)
- **Status**: Accepted
- **Date**: 2026-11-22
- **Owners**: `mobile-agent`, `backend-agent`, `solution-architect`, `security-agent`
- **Context**:
  - Users need to check their hydration progress, remaining goal, daily budget left, streaks, and unlock progress without opening the app, and need actionable notifications to quick-log drinks with one tap.
  - Operations, product, and QA teams need a secure Admin Portal (`/admin`) with God-Mode (all features/animals/biomes/beverages unlocked) and management modules for users, content, monetization, notifications, feature flags, remote config, and security audit logs.
- **Decision**:
  1. **Out-of-App Telemetry & Budget Calculation Engine**:
     - Implement `WidgetNotificationTelemetryService` producing canonical real-time telemetry snapshots:
       * **Water Consumed**: Net milliliters and progress percentage ($P = \text{netMl} / \text{effectiveGoalMl}$).
       * **Remaining Goal**: Exact milliliters needed to reach daily target.
       * **Daily Budgets Left**: Hydration fluid budget (remaining ml), Caffeine budget remaining (max 400mg/day safe limit - consumed caffeine), Electrolyte sodium/potassium balance score.
       * **Gamification Progress**: Active streak count, available streak freezes, current companion evolution stage, and XP needed for next unlock.
  2. **Rich Interactive Notifications**:
     - Format background notifications with dynamic category actions (`+250ml Pure Water`, `+100ml Quick Sip`, `Snooze 30m`, `View Habitat`).
     - Actionable background intents log consumption and update streak counters without requiring the full application UI to launch.
  3. **Universal Home & Lock-Screen Widget Engine**:
     - Create native bridge contracts (`android_widgets`, `ios_widgetkit`, `web_badge`) and build an in-app interactive **Widget Center** allowing users to preview and customize Small (2x2), Medium (4x2), and Lock Screen Live Activity widgets.
  4. **Admin Command Portal (`/admin`)**:
     - Register `/admin` in `app_router.dart` guarded by RBAC permissions (`isAdmin` claim or debug/god-mode bypass).
     - Build a multi-tab administrative workspace:
       * **Overview & Real-Time KPIs**: Active users, total water logged today, ARR/MRR, sync queue health.
       * **God Mode & Instant Overrides**: One-tap master switch to unlock all animals, evolution stages, biomes, beverages, and premium entitlements.
       * **User Management Directory**: Search user profiles, view hydration history, grant XP/streak freeze tokens, inspect sync outbox.
       * **Content Catalog Manager**: CRUD operations on beverages, companions, biomes, and seasonal challenges.
       * **Monetization & SKU Manager**: Price overrides, promotional discount codes, subscription status management.
       * **Notification Broadcast Center**: Compose and dispatch immediate or scheduled push alerts.
       * **Feature Flags & Remote Config**: Dynamic runtime toggles (e.g., `enable_high_precision_waves`, `ai_coach_v2`, `summer_biome_active`).
       * **Environment & Security Audit Logs**: Switch between `Development`, `Staging`, and `Production` with an immutable append-only audit trail logging every admin action.
- **Consequences**: Elevates user retention through ambient lock-screen/widget awareness, enables instant zero-tap hydration tracking, and equips operators with a full enterprise control plane.
